Research Project

Women’s Decision-Making Autonomy as a Mitigating Factor for Complete Antenatal Care Coverage

This research focused on complete antenatal care coverage among women in Bangladesh using BDHS 2017–18 data. The study examined how women’s decision-making autonomy may influence maternal healthcare utilization and access to complete antenatal care services.

The project explored socio-economic, demographic, and healthcare-related factors associated with antenatal care coverage. Special attention was given to women’s autonomy in household and healthcare decisions as an important factor in improving maternal health outcomes.

Data Source: Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey 2017–18

Methods: Descriptive Analysis, Chi-square Test, Regression Analysis

Tools: R, STATA, SPSS
